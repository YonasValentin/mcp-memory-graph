A commercial license is required for any use with anticipated commercial

- Using it inside a for-profit company's products, services, or **internal

- Offering it — or a product/service built on it — to customers, whether hosted

If you are not sure which side of the line your use falls on, ask before you
deploy — it's cheaper than guessing.

## Why this model

The project stays free for individuals, students, researchers, and nonprofits.
Companies that derive commercial value fund continued development and support.
Everything still runs **100% locally** — there is no cloud dependency, telemetry,
or per-token cost in either tier.
